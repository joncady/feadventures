# Independent mobile landscape review — 1.1.3

Reviewed locally on 2026-10-12. The user requested landscape-only phone play because portrait is unusable; the canvas is never rotated or stretched to simulate orientation.

## Verified

- Chromium: three independent landscape-gate checks pass. A fresh 390×844 coarse-pointer device sees a full-viewport rotate prompt; hidden menu keyboard actions cannot advance party selection. Tab and Shift+Tab remain on the visible Fullscreen action; background controls are inert. Rotation to 844×390 restores the initialized leader picker. A narrow 390×844 desktop with a fine pointer remains usable without the gate.
- Real Chromium touch events: rotating during held movement and attack clears both inputs, pauses active battle, and resumes it only after returning to landscape. A battle manually paused before rotation remains paused.
- Existing native regressions: phone fullscreen containment, dual-stick pointer ownership through cardinal and diagonal aiming, cooldown-driven repeated attacks, separate staff ally/self healing, guard and pause, movement cancellation, and command target sizes pass in supported landscape viewports.
- WebKit: fresh portrait startup/rotation and phone fullscreen fallback/viewport-height containment pass. No page errors were recorded in the startup/orientation check.
- Browser orientation lock is attempted only in fullscreen. A rejected mocked lock is safely caught; fullscreen exit calls unlock. This verifies error handling rather than OS rotation policy.
- Typecheck and production build pass. Builder also ran the 69-test unit suite successfully.

The touch fixtures formerly exercised portrait combat. They now use landscape dimensions, keeping their real pointer and gameplay assertions intact rather than bypassing the new gate.

## Limits

These are browser-emulation checks, not physical iPhone or Android observations. Safari may not expose or permit Screen Orientation locking; the rotation prompt is the supported fallback. Actual native fullscreen, browser chrome, OS orientation-lock settings and hardware safe-area insets still need a phone trial. Existing short-screen touch target/layout tradeoffs remain separate from this gate.
