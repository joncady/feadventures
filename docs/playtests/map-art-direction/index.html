What to judge

Does the new coast read as a fortified landing with a connected bridge, staging square and guard post? Do the ruins read as a broken shrine with grounded carved reliefs, a breach and a ritual destination? Do landmarks remain legible around the HUD and thumb areas?

The new coast keeps its bridge and gate district together from a matched authored source. The dry ruins consolidate their shrine facade, collapsed west wing and ritual floor medallion around one clear front approach. Decoration is not a promise of new mechanics; no chest, boat, torch animation or destructible feature is implied.
