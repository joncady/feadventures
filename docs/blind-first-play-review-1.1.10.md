## Priorities

1. Potential blocker to investigate:844×390 viewport cropped lower game and pause actions; scrolling/fullscreen attempts did not recover view. Reproduce on actual phone before treating this as confirmed mobile failure.
2. High-impact onboarding: show a clearly labeled Controls entry or first-battle instruction card before simulation starts. Pause contains essential aiming, weapon, healing, guarding, and rally controls. Rally ready alone did not tell me how to rally. AX-only basic instructions should not be counted as visible human onboarding.
3. Combat clarity: objective east is clear, but red/gold road marker initially looked like a camp/banner. After deployment camps said Four known camps, whereas battle had3 timed waves; not clear if goal requires killing all, waiting all waves, or just reaching exit. Did not reach exit, so completion condition remains unverified.
4. Polish: equipment decision support (power/range/heal values, equipped marker explanation); deployment click/Space hint before first placement; damage50% meaning; tiny overlapping unit name/weapon labels amid crowds.
