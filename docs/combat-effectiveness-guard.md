# Typed combat and optional guard

Local follow-up for #12/#16, October 11, 2026. No version, commit or push by this implementation agent.

The shared `resolveDamage` accepts weapon family separately from physical/magical damage kind and effectiveness tags. Both controlled-player and allied/enemy damage paths use it. Explicit magical swords would check Resistance without changing their triangle family. Current weapons retain their reviewed +/-20% triangle, x2 final effectiveness, minimum-one damage and global damage scaling. This deliberately differs from [Awakening calculations](https://serenesforest.net/awakening/miscellaneous/calculations/), where rank affects hit/attack and effectiveness triples Might. This slice does not add random rank-based hit rolls or change existing weapon damage.

Wind is a class-valid shop tome for 90 gold, with 26 power, 340 range, 650ms cooldown and an explicit flying tag. [Awakening's tome table](https://serenesforest.net/awakening/inventory/tomes/) identifies wind-family flyer effectiveness. Ordinary Fire/Thunder do not acquire that tag, nor does all magic become armor-effective. Current Wind reuses the existing source Fire bolt with green tint, tome icon and provisional spell effect; no dedicated Wind art/audio identity is claimed. Projectiles retain their weapon ID at launch, so swapping equipment cannot rewrite their effectiveness in flight. Damage numbers mark an effective hit with a small star.

Hold Control, right mouse or the touch shield to guard. Shift remains self-heal. Guard is an intentional real-time adaptation rather than Awakening's automatic Dual Guard. A 120-degree frontal arc reduces physical melee/arrow damage by 65%; rear hits and magic bypass it. Movement falls to 45%, attacking is excluded, and existing action windup cannot be cancelled by guarding. The meter starts at 100, drains 30/sec plus 18 per blocked hit, and recovers 22/sec. A broken guard needs at least 30 meter and a release before reengaging. Pause freezes game-clock resource changes; new scenes and hero swaps reset input/state. Guard feedback is a rendered shield arc/meter using existing idle art and a quiet existing confirmation clip on impact. No dedicated guard sprite was verified.

Pure tests cover all prior triangle/bow matchups, explicit magical protection, item-specific/empty effectiveness, critical/triangle/effectiveness stacking, front/back/wrapped cones, magic bypass, sustained break/recovery/release, impact depletion and independent fresh states. Focused combat/guard/resolver checks: 13 passed. Strict TypeScript passed after runtime integration. `guard-effectiveness.spec.ts` supplies native Control/movement/Space exclusion plus front/rear/magic, restart and Wind snapshot fixtures; independent reviewer owns their browser run.

Remaining wider combat work: real weapon proficiency/rank progression, new armor/cavalry/dragon-effective inventory, distinct Wind assets, named commander assets/behaviors, and natural campaign balance playtesting. Those are not silently represented as complete by adding typed tags.
