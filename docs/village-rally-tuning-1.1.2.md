# Village pressure and rally handoff — local 1.1.2

Village had the same base raider health as the Road despite persistent stat growth and weapon forging. It now uses fixed authored health ×1.35 and attack power ×1.55 (previously ×1.25). Nothing reads the party's current upgrade level: a forge remains an advantage, rather than triggering an equal enemy increase. Damage still passes through normal defense, triangle, guard and the existing global damage setting; these are attack-power multipliers, not guaranteed HP loss.

Regular soldiers: 60 → 81 HP, 13 → 16 attack. Axemen: 80 → 108 HP, 20 → 25 attack. Archers: 50 → 68 HP, 10 → 12 attack. Mages: 45 → 61 HP, 18 → 22 attack. Knights: 75 → 101 HP, 15 → 19 attack. Two basic axemen slots become one knight (east) and one mage (south), giving persistent defense and ranged pressure rather than more simultaneous bodies. Two waves / 32 scheduled raiders / 44-second minimum wave windows / 14 active enemies remain unchanged. Gangrel retains his authored 700 HP and 32 attack, including his telegraph and reinforcement behavior.

On rally expiry, each companion keeps a fixed engagement anchor at their gathered position while a living enemy remains within the ordinary 300px companion AOE. This is spatially bounded: retreating enemies outside that pocket are not chased. When clear, the original deployment post resumes at 45–75% of the companion's regular movement speed. An approaching enemy within 140px can interrupt that return and start another bounded local engagement. Reaching within 32px of the post ends the temporary return state. Soldiers are unaffected; follow-map companions retain ordinary formation behavior.

Verification: typecheck and all 67 unit tests pass. `tests/e2e/rally-handoff.spec.ts` checks native scene target retention, cleared/far-away threats, return speed and return interruption; independent reviewer owns execution and natural fresh-vs-upgraded Village trials. This document does not claim measured difficulty until those trials are complete.
